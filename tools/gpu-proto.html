GPU rasterizer prototype — CPU vs WebGL2

Captures each style's exact stroke list from the real worker, then rasterizes that identical list two ways — the CPU renderStrokeSolid math and a WebGL2 instanced-capsule shader — over the same white base. The CPU↔GPU delta (and differing hashes) is the fidelity/determinism signal; the timing columns are the speed signal. Solid strokes only; textured/impasto excluded by design. Serve the repo root (npx serve .) and open /tools/gpu-proto.html.
